# Termination / resignation research map
Use for dismissal, unilateral termination, negotiated termination, resignation, alleged coerced resignation, redundancy/economic layoff, or "do not come to work" scenarios.

Verify, as applicable: who initiated termination; exact reason; notice and evidence; contract status; service length; wage basis; special protected circumstances; final wage/leave/bonus issues; local rules; and current judicial interpretations.

Research authoritative sources before citing article numbers. Separate eligibility for economic compensation, damages/compensation for unlawful termination, notice-related amounts, and other independent claims. Do not assume "N", "N+1", or "2N" applies from the user's label alone.
