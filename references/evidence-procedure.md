# Evidence / procedure research map
Use whenever a dispute may progress to negotiation, mediation, arbitration, or litigation.

Build a chronology and evidence table. Typical categories may include contracts, offer/onboarding records, payroll/bank records, attendance, social-insurance records, work-product records, internal policies, notices, chat/email, signed forms, and lawful recordings.

Never guarantee admissibility. Distinguish evidence possessed by the worker, evidence likely controlled by the employer, and evidence obtainable from official records. Verify limitation periods, jurisdiction, filing requirements, and burden-of-proof rules from current authoritative sources.
