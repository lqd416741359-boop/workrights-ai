# Preferred research sources
Use official/current sources wherever available.

Priority domains/categories:
- National People's Congress / national legislation resources: npc.gov.cn and official national legislation database resources
- State Council / central government: gov.cn
- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security: mohrss.gov.cn
- Supreme People's Court and court gazette: court.gov.cn, gongbao.court.gov.cn
- Provincial/municipal government, HRSS, and court official websites for local rules/practice

Source discipline:
- Do not use search snippets as the legal authority itself.
- Open and verify the underlying official page/document.
- Check promulgation/effective/amendment/repeal status when material.
- Secondary commentary may help discover an issue but should not replace official authority for a material proposition when an official source exists.
